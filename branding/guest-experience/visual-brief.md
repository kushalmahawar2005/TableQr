## Prompt set

Shared direction: Premium photorealistic lifestyle photograph for a restaurant technology brochure. Landscape 3:2. Modern Indian restaurant with cream interiors and subtle rose/red accents; natural restaurant lighting and appetizing food photography. Smartphone screens show cream and orange ordering UI with actual food photos. No headlines or watermarks; believable hands and skin.

1. Scan and explore: Over a diner’s shoulder, show a black smartphone with the opened food menu beside a table QR stand. Paneer tikka and butter chicken appear on the screen; plated paneer tikka sits on the wooden table. Focus on the phone and QR stand. Minimal readable screen labels: Menu, Paneer Tikka, Add.
2. Order together: Two young Indian friends, a woman and a man, choose dishes together on one smartphone. Show orange Add buttons and food photos. Warm restaurant atmosphere, paneer tikka, butter naan and a drink on the table. Emphasize shared ordering, not eating or toasting.
3. Ask for service: Foreground smartphone with an orange Call waiter button on a cream screen. An Indian waiter in a white shirt and muted rose apron approaches with water, softly out of focus. Screen labels: Service, Water, Call waiter. Clear restaurant setting with food on the table.
