Ask us on WhatsApp
Do guests need to download an app?

No. Guests scan a table or room QR code and open the guest experience in their phone browser. The menu supports English and Hindi.

Can I use Tableeqr for a hotel as well as a restaurant?

Yes. Tableeqr includes room QR codes, food ordering, housekeeping and guest requests, reception, check-in, check-out and room service bills. It supports hotels with or without a restaurant.

Can guests reserve a table and preorder food?

Yes. Guests can select their time and party size, share seating preferences and preorder dishes. Your team can also manage waitlists and party enquiries.

How do I get Tableeqr set up for my business?

Message Craftory Innovation on WhatsApp. We will walk through your restaurant or hotel, show you the relevant workflows and help you choose the setup for your business.
